How to Play

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe! This classic game is played on a 3x3 grid. Players take turns marking their symbols to get three in a row.

Game Objective

Be the first player to get three of your marks (X or O) in a row, column, or diagonal to win the game.

How to Play

  • Player X starts first, then players alternate turns
  • Click on any empty cell to place your mark
  • Your mark will appear in the selected cell
  • Continue taking turns until someone wins or the board is full

Winning Conditions

You win by getting three of your marks in any of these patterns:

  • Three in a row horizontally
  • Three in a column vertically
  • Three in a diagonal line

Game Controls

  • Help: Click the "Help" button in the top-right to reopen this help menu
  • Update Names: Click "Update Name" on any player card to change player names
  • Restart: Click "Restart" to reset the game and scores
  • Play Again: Click "Play Again" after a game ends to start a new round

Game End

The game ends when:

  • A player gets three marks in a row (that player wins)
  • All cells are filled with no winner (it's a draw)
